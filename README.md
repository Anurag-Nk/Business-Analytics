# Business-Analytics
# Restaurant Business Analytics Dashboard

---

A comprehensive, interactive **Power BI Business Intelligence Dashboard** built to analyze restaurant, customer, revenue, and delivery data. The dashboard provides actionable insights into restaurant performance, customer behavior, operational efficiency, and overall business growth through six interactive report pages.

---

# 📖 Short Description / Purpose

The **Business Analytics Dashboard** is an end-to-end Power BI project designed to transform raw restaurant and delivery data into meaningful business insights. It enables business stakeholders to monitor revenue, customer trends, restaurant performance, delivery operations, and advanced business metrics through interactive visualizations and KPI-driven reports.

The dashboard is ideal for **business analysts, restaurant managers, delivery operations teams, executives, and data enthusiasts** looking to make data-driven decisions.

---

# 🛠 Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

- 📊 **Power BI Desktop** – Primary dashboard development and visualization platform.
- 📂 **Power Query** – Data cleaning, transformation, and preprocessing.
- 🧠 **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – Calculated columns, KPIs, dynamic measures, and business logic.
- 🗄️ **Data Modeling** – Relationship modeling between restaurant, customer, order, delivery, and city tables.
- 🎨 **Custom Theme & UI Design** – Corporate Blue color palette with modern business dashboard layout.
- 📁 **File Format** – `.pbix` for development and `.png` for dashboard previews.

---

The dataset contains detailed information regarding:

- Restaurants
- Orders
- Customers
- Delivery Details
- Revenue
- Ratings
- Cities
- Cuisine Types
- Customer Types
- Order Status
- Cost Buckets

The data was cleaned, transformed, and modeled using Power Query before creating relationships and DAX measures.

# ✨ Features / Highlights

## 📌 Business Problem

Restaurant businesses generate enormous volumes of operational and customer data every day. However, understanding revenue performance, delivery efficiency, customer retention, restaurant quality, and operational bottlenecks from raw data is extremely difficult.

Business leaders frequently need answers to questions such as:

- Which cities generate the highest revenue?
- Which restaurants perform the best?
- Are delivery operations efficient?
- How many customers are repeat customers?
- Which customer segment places the most orders?
- Which restaurants require operational improvements?

Without an interactive dashboard, answering these questions becomes time-consuming.

---

## 🎯 Goal of the Dashboard

The dashboard aims to:

- Monitor overall business performance.
- Analyze restaurant revenue and customer behavior.
- Track delivery efficiency.
- Identify high-performing restaurants.
- Compare city-wise business metrics.
- Improve operational decision-making.
- Provide executives with real-time KPIs.

# 📊 Dashboard Walkthrough

## 1️⃣ Executive Overview

Provides a high-level summary of the entire business.

### Key KPIs

- 💰 Total Revenue
- 📦 Total Orders
- 💵 Average Order Value
- 🚚 Average Delivery Time

### Visuals

- Revenue Trend
- Orders Trend
- Revenue by City
- Order Status Breakdown
- Top 10 Restaurants
- City Filter

Business users can quickly understand the overall health of the business from this page.

---

## 2️⃣ Customer Analysis

Focuses on customer behavior and engagement.

### Key Metrics

- Total Customers
- Repeat Customers
- Repeat Customer %
- Orders per Customer

### Visuals

- New vs Repeat Customers
- Orders by Customer Type
- Signup Trend
- Top Restaurants Table
- Table Booking Analysis

Insights generated:

- Customer retention
- Customer acquisition
- Loyalty trends
- Booking preferences

---

## 3️⃣ Restaurant Performance

Analyzes restaurant quality and business contribution.

### KPIs

- Total Restaurants
- Average Rating
- Average Votes
- Revenue per Restaurant

### Visuals

- Revenue by Top Restaurants
- Average Rating by Cuisine
- Orders by Cuisine
- Restaurant Rating Distribution
- Cost Bucket vs Orders

Useful for identifying:

- Top-performing restaurants
- Popular cuisines
- Revenue leaders
- Restaurant quality trends

---

## 4️⃣ Delivery & Operations

Tracks operational performance of deliveries.

### KPIs

- Average Delivery Time
- Late Orders
- Late Delivery %
- Cancelled Orders

### Visuals

- Delivery Status Breakdown
- Cancellation by City
- City-wise Delivery Time
- Monthly Delivery Analysis
- Delivery Operations Table

Business impact:

- Detect delivery bottlenecks
- Improve logistics
- Reduce cancellations

---
